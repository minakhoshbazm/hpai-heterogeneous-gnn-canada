## Epidemiological Classification & Terminology Policy
1. **Full National Cohort (5,054 CSDs)**: Represents national risk forecasting across all Canadian CSDs.
2. **GLW Poultry Presence Cohort (4,471 CSDs)**: Uses GLW2020 gridded chicken density > 0 as a **poultry-presence proxy**. Eliminates 583 zero-poultry wilderness CSDs. Retains 100.0% of national outbreaks.
3. **Primary Commercial Poultry Cohort (2,222 CSDs)**: Defined as `ccs_farms_reporting > 0 OR ccs_production_kg > 0`. Because Agricultural Census features represent static regional farm inventory rather than real-time monthly operational status, this cohort is strictly titled **Commercial Poultry Presence Cohort** rather than active-farm cohort.
